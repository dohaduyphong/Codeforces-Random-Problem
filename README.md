# Codeforces Random Problem

Pick a rating range and get a random Codeforces problem to practice.

## Features

- Set a min and max rating (800 to 3500) and get a random problem in that range.
- Enter your Codeforces handle to skip problems you've already solved.
- Limit picks to Div. 1, Div. 2, Div. 3, Div. 4, Div. 1 + 2, Educational, Global or other rounds. Leave all unchecked for any contest.
- Filter by tags such as `dp` or `greedy`. A problem must have every selected tag.
- A problem's tags stay hidden until you click "Show tags", so they don't give away the approach.
- Your last 10 problems are listed under Recent picks, and they won't come up again until the filters run out of other problems.
- Press `R` to roll again. You can turn the shortcut off.
- Light and dark themes follow your system setting, with a toggle in the header.
